## What changed
- **`docs/v2/ADR-0008-background-helper.md`** (new) decides two things.
  - **v2.0:** in-app "Keep watching in the background" (off by default). After the panel closes,
    Live keeps sampling at the adaptive cadence, so alerts and history keep working, and Quit
    stops it.
  - **v2.1:** an `SMAppService` LaunchAgent (`Contents/Helpers/heatlens-helper`, started on
    demand, with no `RunAtLoad` and no `KeepAlive`). Both releases run the same core
    `BackgroundMonitor`.

  The ADR also covers:
  - **IPC:** two XPC Mach services (control for the app, read-only for the CLI), each checked
    against a code-signing requirement on every connection. Messages are typed, versioned
    `Codable` envelopes in binary plist, with an 8 MB cap. ADR-0003's "no local server" holds.
  - **What runs where:** sampling, rules, alerts and history move to the helper. The UI,
    notifications, one-off scans and every action stay in the app. The helper never signals and
    never writes to Docker, so ADR-0006's "Revisit when" doesn't trigger. One history writer at
    a time: the helper owns the file while it runs, and the app sends it action records and
    Delete history.
  - **Lifecycle:** a lifecycle table (Quit pauses until the app opens; after an app crash the
    helper keeps sampling for at most 1 hour), the bundle layout and an uninstall leftovers
    table.
  - **Signing:** no entitlements; `release.sh`'s Helpers loop already signs the new executable.
  - **Overhead budgets:** ≤ 0.3 % of one core idle on power, ≤ 0.1 % in Low Power Mode, ≤ 2 %
    during an incident, helper ≤ 25 MB.
  - **Security review:** a 13-row threat table with proofs.
  - **Options considered**, and build briefs **4b, 4c** (v2.0) and **4j, 4k** (v2.1).
- **`docs/v2/PHASE-4-PLAN.md`** (new) holds:
  - the nine owner decisions and the order of every brief, 4a–4l;
  - a design and build brief for adaptive sampling (4a), structured logs (4d), crash recovery
    (4e), watchlists (4f/4g), incident export (4h/4i) and the MCP live tools (4l, v2.1);
  - a table of the PRD Phase 4 items not covered, and which still need product decisions:
    notification snooze/frequency and a menu-bar icon change. Auto-update is deferred.
- **`docs/v2/PLAN.md`**: a "Designed" paragraph under Phase 4 only, linking both docs.
